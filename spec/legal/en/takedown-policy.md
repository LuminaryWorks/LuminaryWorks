# Takedown policy (engineering template)

**NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** Engineering template for counsel, version `lw-legal-v2026-11-07`. Have a lawyer admitted in France review it before it is published as a binding procedure. It is a platform process for partner products. It is not legal advice, and it does not make LuminaryWorks the author or the operator of partner content.

Platform contact: admin@luminaryworks.dev. The partner operator of a named product is {{PARTNER_OPERATOR_NAME}} until the real legal name is written into the signed partner agreement. Product-content questions go to that operator as well as to the platform.

## 1. What a notice must contain

A notice about material available through the LuminaryWorks Platform must include:

1. The notifier's name, mailing or email address, and a statement that the notifier is the rights holder or is authorized to act for the rights holder.
2. The partner product name.
3. The URL or a description specific enough to find the material.
4. The work or right claimed, and the basis of the claim.
5. A good-faith statement that the use is not authorized by the rights holder, its agent, or the law.
6. A statement that the information is accurate, and that the notifier is authorized to act, under penalty of perjury where that standard applies.

Incomplete notices may be rejected. Send notices to admin@luminaryworks.dev. Also send a copy to the partner operator if that operator publishes a contact on its own site.

## 2. What the platform may do

After a notice that meets section 1, LuminaryWorks may disable the identified material, or suspend that partner product's listing, hosted access, or checkout. The action stays on the named partner product. Other partner products and the platform account service stay up, unless the same notice identifies them with the same specificity.

LuminaryWorks tells the partner operator, at the address in the partner agreement, what was disabled. LuminaryWorks does not decide ownership of the content by taking that step.

## 3. Counter-notice

The partner operator, or the user who posted the material if the product's terms say the user is responsible, may send a counter-notice to admin@luminaryworks.dev. It must include:

1. The sender's name, address, and email.
2. Identification of the material and where it appeared.
3. A good-faith statement that the material was disabled by mistake or misidentification.
4. Consent to the venue stated in the partner agreement, or, if none is signed yet, a statement that the sender will accept service of process from the notifier.
5. A signature (physical or electronic).

If the counter-notice is complete, LuminaryWorks forwards it to the notifier. Unless the notifier starts a court action and tells the platform within 10 business days, LuminaryWorks may restore the material. Restoration is not a finding that the material is lawful.

## 4. Repeat infringer

If a partner product is the subject of more than one valid notice that is not undone by a counter-notice or a court outcome, LuminaryWorks may suspend that partner under the kill switch in the partner agreement. Repeat-infringer handling applies to that partner product. It is not applied to other partners, and it is not applied to the platform as a whole.

## 5. Misrepresentation and other complaints

A person who knowingly misrepresents that material is infringing, or that it was disabled by mistake, may be liable under applicable law. LuminaryWorks may ignore notices that are incomplete, abusive, or unrelated to material on the platform.

Complaints that are not intellectual-property notices (privacy, consumer, product defects) go to the partner operator for product behavior, and to admin@luminaryworks.dev for the platform account, billing, or entitlement.
